Kalamang Grammar Rules:
1. Sentence Structure: 
   - The basic structure follows Subject-Verb-Object (SVO) order.
   Example: "Saya makan nasi." -> "I eat rice."

2. Questions: 
   - To form a question, add the particle "ka" at the end of the sentence.
   Example: "Kamu datang ka?" -> "Are you coming?"

3. Greetings: 
   - "Halo" -> "Hello"
   - "Selamat pagi" -> "Good morning"
   - "Selamat malam" -> "Good night"

4. Negation: 
   - Use the word "tidak" before a verb to negate it.
   Example: "Saya tidak tahu." -> "I don't know."

5. Plurals: 
   - Reduplicate the noun to indicate plurality.
   Example: "Orang" (person) -> "Orang-orang" (people)

6. Possessives: 
   - Use "punya" followed by a pronoun to express possession.
   Example: "Ini buku saya." -> "This is my book."

7. Tenses: 
   - Use time-related words since there are no inflectional tenses.
   Example: "Saya makan tadi." -> "I ate earlier."
   Example: "Saya akan makan." -> "I will eat."

8. Pronouns: 
   - "Saya" -> "I"
   - "Kamu" -> "You"
   - "Dia" -> "He/She"
   - "Kami" -> "We (exclusive)"
   - "Kita" -> "We (inclusive)"

9. Common Conjunctions: 
   - "Dan" -> "And"
   - "Atau" -> "Or"
   - "Tetapi" -> "But"

10. Adjectives: 
   - Adjectives follow the noun.
   Example: "Rumah besar" -> "Big house"
